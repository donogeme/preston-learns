What This Teaches

The Magic E (or "Silent E") rule: when a word ends in vowel-consonant-E, the E is silent but makes the vowel "say its name."

  • cap → cape (A says "ay")
  • hop → hope (O says "oh")
  • kit → kite (I says "eye")

Learning Progression

  1. Discover: Finn shows word transformations
  2. Predict: Child adds the Magic E themselves
  3. Sort: Identify which words have Magic E

How to Help

  • Exaggerate vowel sounds: "c-AAA-p" vs "c-AY-pe"
  • In real life, point out Magic E words (cake, bike, home)
  • Play "What if we add Magic E?" with CVC words
