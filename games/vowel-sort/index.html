📚 For Parents

What This Teaches

This game develops phonemic awareness — the ability to hear and distinguish vowel sounds in words.

Preston is learning to tell the difference between:

  • Short A (/ă/) — the "ah" sound in "cat", "hat", "map"
  • Long A (/ā/) — the "ay" sound in "cake", "rain", "play"

How to Help

  • Tap the buckets together to compare sounds
  • Exaggerate the vowel: "c-AAA-t" vs "c-AY-ke"
  • No worries about wrong answers — that's learning!
  • First session is 5 words, then increases to 10
